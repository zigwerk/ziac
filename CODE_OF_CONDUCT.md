# Zigwerk Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous and welcoming
community.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be kind, direct and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different experience levels, identities and communication styles.
- Give credit and preserve authorship.
- Treat security reports and private information responsibly.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree
  constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, deliberate disruption,
doxxing and sustained bad-faith behaviour are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments and accounts that violate
this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or
permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately to the organisation owners.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behaviour and the
safety of affected people. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
